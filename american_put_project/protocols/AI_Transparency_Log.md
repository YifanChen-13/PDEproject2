# AI transparency

The user selected the perpetual American put direction and requested a finished,
executed project. AI authored the draft model, solver, experiment driver, automated
checks, report text and figure layout, and executed the supplied experiments.

Verification includes hand-row checks, exhaustive small-grid active-set comparison,
four-level analytic convergence, a refined-grid reference, independent ODE shooting,
physical inequalities, contact-set checks, solver-tolerance and domain studies.

Development correction: the original exact-tie policy selected exercise in the
zero-payoff tail, causing unnecessarily slow one-node propagation. Exact ties now
select continuation. Final results and prediction logs were regenerated from the
final solver. This is an algorithmic efficiency correction, not a modified model.

Predictions are AI-authored and recorded before the final production experiments;
they are not represented as independent student predictions made before development.

The exact official five-gate audit and precomputation worksheet templates were not
available. The custom audit documents evidence under five headings but does not
assert completion of the official forms. Group members' review, contribution
statements and course-required declarations remain their own responsibility.
